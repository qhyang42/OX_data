# experiment design and project info for codex/chatgpt 

## Task design 
Participants were asked to rate the pleasantness of odors under four different contexts: person, food, location, and none (control condition). For each trial, participants were given a brief audio description of an imaginative scenario in which the odor was encountered (eg. 'You shake hands with a man, and a smell enters your nose'), followed by a sniff prompt ('3, 2, 1, sniff'), and they were asked to rate the pleasantness and the intensity. 
### trial structure 
`+ ---> visual and auditory cue word ('person') ---> audio story cue ('You shake hands with a man, and a smell enters your nose') ---> sniff prompt ('3, 2, 1, sniff')  ---> rating ('how pleasant was the person/how strong was the odor')`

## Subjects and stimuli. 
5 completed subjects. 20 odors used across 4 contexts. 800 trials across 80 runs.
